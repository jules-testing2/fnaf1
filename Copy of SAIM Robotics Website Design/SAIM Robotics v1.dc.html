About

Systems for Autonomous Intelligent Mobility

SAIM Robotics is a Romanian robotics lab building physically autonomous systems — mobile robots, humanoid and quadruped platforms, and manipulation arms — unified by one perception-to-action software stack. Our mission is to make autonomy portable across bodies, not locked to one machine.

We work with industrial, defense, and field-research partners who need robots that understand what they see well enough to act on it without a human in the loop.
