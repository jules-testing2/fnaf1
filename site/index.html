What SAIM does

FIIR · POLITEHNICA Bucharest · RO

We build robots that understand what they see well enough to act on it. Wheels, legs or arms, every SAIM body shares one Semantic Autonomy Stack: it finds objects, places them on a map, routes around what they mean and works out where an instruction should lead. One stack. Every body.

The Semantic Autonomy Stack

One stack. Every body.

About the lab

Systems for Autonomous Intelligent Mobility.

SAIM Robotics is a Romanian robotics lab at the Faculty of Industrial Engineering and Robotics (FIIR), National University of Science and Technology POLITEHNICA Bucharest. It began as a class of innovation where students learn by building things in house.

Our mission: make autonomy portable across bodies, not locked to one machine.

We work with industrial, defense and field-research partners who need robots that understand what they see well enough to act on it, without a human in the loop.
