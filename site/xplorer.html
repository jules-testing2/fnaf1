What Xplorer sees

From pixels and ranges to a route that thinks.

On the research robots everything below runs on a single Raspberry Pi 5, CPU only, under ROS 2 Jazzy and Nav2.

  1. 01 · Camera

    Detect

    YOLO26n on NCNN finds people, chairs, plants and more at 5.5 ± 0.7 FPS on the Pi.

  2. 02 · 2D LiDAR

    Range

    Angular Sector Fusion projects each box onto LiDAR rays and takes the 25th-percentile range.

  3. 03 · Semantic map

    Remember

    Objects are classified as dynamic, static or minor and stored in a GeoJSON semantic map.

  4. 04 · Nav2 Route Server

    Replan

    Penalties heat up graph edges; past a threshold the route is recomputed around the problem.
